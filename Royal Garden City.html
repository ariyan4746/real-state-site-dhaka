About the company

A property company that stays for the whole project.

Royal Garden City works across the life of a property in Dhaka. We help clients find and buy land or a home. We develop and build. Our interior studio then finishes the space. Keeping these disciplines together means one budget, one schedule and one team that answers for the result.

Dhaka rewards people who understand its plots, its approval routes and its building seasons. We plan around them from the first meeting, and we tell clients early when something will cost more or take longer.

How we work

Five steps from first call to handover.

  1. Step 1

    Consultation

    We listen to your brief, budget and timeline, and say plainly what is realistic.

  2. Step 2

    Site and feasibility

    We check the land, its records and the planning rules before design begins.

  3. Step 3

    Design and approvals

    Architecture, layouts and interiors are drawn together, then submitted for approval.

  4. Step 4

    Construction and fit-out

    Our site team builds and finishes to the agreed scope, with regular progress reports.

  5. Step 5

    Handover and care

    We inspect with you, hand over documents and keys, and stay available afterwards.
